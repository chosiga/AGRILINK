Privacy Notice

This notice describes the account and marketplace information AgriLink uses to operate the service.

Information you provide

When you register, AgriLink stores your name, email address, phone number, location, account role, and a password hash. Marketplace activity such as listings, orders, delivery requests, notifications, and reviews is stored to provide the corresponding features.

How information is used

Account and activity information is used to run the marketplace, display public farmer profiles and listings, coordinate orders and deliveries, protect accounts, and respond to support requests.

Some contact details are shared with the other party after an order or delivery request reaches the relevant stage, to enable users to complete the transaction.

Security and retention

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and session tokens are not stored in plain text. The service uses account data for as long as needed to operate marketplace features and maintain associated records.

Your choices

Contact AgriLink to ask a question about your account information or request help. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or secret keys in a support message.
