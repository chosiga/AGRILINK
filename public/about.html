About AgriLink Zambia

AgriLink Zambia is an online marketplace that connects farmers, buyers and transporters across the country.

Our Mission

To help farmers sell their produce at fair prices, give buyers easy access to fresh products, and make delivery simple and reliable.

What We Stand For

  • Fair Prices

    Farmers deal directly with buyers, so more of the money stays with them.

  • Fresh Produce

    Products move quickly from the farm to the buyer.

  • Reliable Delivery

    Trusted transporters get produce where it needs to go.
