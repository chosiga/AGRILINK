Terms of Use

These terms explain the basic rules for using AgriLink Zambia. By creating an account or using the marketplace, you agree to follow them.

Marketplace relationships

AgriLink helps farmers, buyers, and transporters find one another. Buyers and sellers are responsible for checking product details, quantities, quality, prices, and delivery arrangements before agreeing to a trade.

Listings and availability are provided by sellers and may change. Do not list goods you are not authorised to sell, and do not provide misleading information.

Accounts and conduct

Keep your sign-in details private, provide accurate account information, and use only the account role that reflects your activity. Contact AgriLink if you believe your account has been misused.

Payments and delivery

A payment method selected on an order is a preference only. AgriLink does not currently process payments or hold funds. Buyers and sellers must agree payment terms directly and should confirm them before transferring money.

Delivery requests help users coordinate with transporters. Agree on delivery cost, timing, and handling directly with the transporter before dispatch.

Reviews and verification

Reviews may be submitted only for eligible, completed marketplace orders. Farmer verification is an AgriLink account status and is not a guarantee of product quality or performance.

Questions

For questions or to report a concern, contact the AgriLink team.
